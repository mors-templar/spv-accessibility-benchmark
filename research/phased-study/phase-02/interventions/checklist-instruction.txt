Use the accessibility checklist below while you complete the request above.
Keep all required features from the original request.
Apply each item only when it is relevant to the requested site.
R items summarize conditional WCAG outcomes and have exceptions.
G items describe guidance or possible techniques; their underlying outcomes may still be required.
P items describe research activities rather than mandatory site features.
Do not claim a check passed unless you have observed supporting evidence.
Do not call a missing requested feature not applicable.
Mark any item you cannot assess as unassessed.
Create an accessibility self-report for every checklist item ID.
For each R or G item, record pass, fail, not applicable, or unassessed.
A G-item pass means the guidance was applied; it does not prove WCAG conformance.
A G-item fail means the guidance was not applied; it is not automatically a WCAG failure.
For each P item, record research activity and do not call it a site defect.
Give a short reason and evidence for each status.
If you cannot create a file, include the report in your response.
The independent study team will review the finished site separately.
